# Northwind Robotics — Vacation and Leave Policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year.
Part-time employees receive leave pro rata to their contracted hours.
Leave accrues monthly at a rate of 2 days per month worked.

Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year. Carried-over days
expire on March 31 if they are not used.

## Requesting leave

Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 14 calendar days in advance
for periods longer than 3 days. Shorter leave requires 3 days notice.
Requests are approved by the direct manager within 2 working days.

## Sick leave

Employees get 10 days of paid sick leave per year without a doctor's note.
From the 3rd consecutive sick day a medical certificate is required.
Sick leave does not reduce annual leave.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Parental leave must start within 12 months of the birth or adoption.
