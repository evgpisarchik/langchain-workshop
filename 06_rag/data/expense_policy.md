# Northwind Robotics — Travel and Expense Policy

## General rules

All business expenses must be submitted in the Expensify app within 30 days,
together with a photo of the receipt. Expenses without receipts above 25 EUR are not reimbursed.
Reimbursement is paid with the next monthly salary.

## Travel

Economy class is required for flights under 6 hours. Business class is allowed
for flights longer than 6 hours with approval from a department head.
Trains are preferred over flights for trips under 4 hours.

Hotels are reimbursed up to 150 EUR per night in most cities and up to 220 EUR per night
in London, Paris, New York, Zurich and Tokyo.

## Meals

The daily meal allowance during business travel is 60 EUR.
Alcohol is not reimbursable, except at client dinners approved in advance.

## Equipment

Employees may expense home-office equipment up to 500 EUR once every two years.
Laptops and phones are provided by IT and must not be purchased personally.
