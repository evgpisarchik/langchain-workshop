# Northwind Robotics — Product FAQ (Rover R2)

## What is the Rover R2?

The Rover R2 is an autonomous warehouse robot that moves shelves of up to 800 kg.
It navigates using lidar and floor QR markers and integrates with most WMS systems via REST API.

## Battery and charging

The battery lasts 10 hours under typical load. A full charge takes 90 minutes
at a docking station. The robot returns to the dock automatically below 15% charge.

## Pricing and warranty

The list price is 38,000 EUR per unit. Volume discounts start at 10 units.
The standard warranty is 2 years; an extended 5-year warranty costs 4,500 EUR per unit.

## Safety

The Rover R2 is certified to ISO 3691-4. It stops within 0.5 m when a person is detected
in its path and its maximum speed is 2 m/s.
