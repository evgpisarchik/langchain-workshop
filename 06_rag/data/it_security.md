# Northwind Robotics — IT and Security Guidelines

## Passwords and accounts

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Use the company password manager (1Password).
Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN, GitHub and the HR portal.
Never share accounts; every person has their own credentials.

## Devices

Company laptops use full-disk encryption and must lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@northwind.example within 1 hour.
Personal devices may access email only through the managed mobile app.

## Data classification

Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted.
Customer data and source code are Confidential. Payroll and medical data are Restricted.
Restricted data must never be pasted into external AI tools or chat services.

## VPN and remote work

VPN is required when working from public Wi-Fi. The VPN client is WireGuard,
configured automatically on company laptops. Support is available via the #it-help Slack channel.
